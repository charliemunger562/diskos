### Is diskos safe to use?
Yes! diskos is designed to work safely with your FiiO Snowsky. However, since it's a beta version, we recommend backing up any important files on your device first.

### What if something goes wrong?
Don't panic! Here are some troubleshooting tips:

- **Program won't start:** Make sure you extracted all files from the ZIP
- **Device not detected:** Try a different USB cable or port
- **Installation fails:** Restart your computer and try again
- **Player not working:** Disconnect and reconnect your device

### How do I update diskos?
Check the download page regularly for new versions. Download the latest ZIP and repeat the installation process.

## 🛠️ Troubleshooting Common Issues

### Issue: "Windows protected your PC" message
This is normal for new software. Click **"More info"** and then **"Run anyway"** to proceed.

### Issue: USB device not recognized
1. Try a different USB port on your computer
2. Use the original USB cable that came with your FiiO
3. Restart your computer
4. Make sure your device is fully charged

### Issue: Installation gets stuck
1. Wait at least 5 minutes (it can be slow sometimes)
2. If still stuck, disconnect your device and try again
3. Make sure no other programs are using your USB ports
